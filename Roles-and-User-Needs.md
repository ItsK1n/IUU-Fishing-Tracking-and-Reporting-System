<h1> Roles and User Needs of the System.</h1> 
The following describes the details of the IUU fish tracking system to detect its roles, users, and needs.

<h1> Role 1: Data Engineers </h1> 

<h3> Purpose and Goals: </h3>

- Construct a secure database.
- Designate a time frame to delete data.
- Allow for prosecutors and analysts to communicate about the needs of data infrastructure.

<h3> Skills & Skill Requirements: </h3>

- Database design and development 
- Backup and recovery
- Scripting and automation
- Data entry
- Encryption of data

<h3> Work Activities & Tasks: </h3>

- Connect with prosecutors to find data needs and what information needs to be stored
- Encode data and construct database
- Optimizing data for collection and making infrastructure

<h3> Task Characteristics: </h2>

- The task of constructing the database, lower frequency of smaller tasks but working on full task
- There is a deadline associated with the task, as the database needs to be constructed to support prosecution efforts
- Tasks are often performed in collaboration with other data engineers/architects
- Tasks focus on streamlining data 
- There is high risk related to the tasks, as data cannot be deleted if critical to process
- User is dedicated to the task at hand (no multitasking)
- Information used: process type of data to choose what needs to be stored

<h3> Needs Related to the New System: </h3>

- Store data within the new database
- Transfer existing data into database
- Structured and coherent data 

<h1> Role 2: Data Analysts</h1> 

<h3> Purpose and Goals: </h3>

- Analyze evidence of IUU fishing on a global scale
- Generate Reports based on evidence of IUU fishing
- Work with prosecutors to present findings and critical evidence
- Analyze seasonal fishing patterns to prevent IUU fishing

<h3> Skills & Skill Requirements: </h3>

- Synthesize data in order to show historical trends of IUU fishing
- Querying data 
- Data visualization
- Ability to make clear and cohesive reports
- Communication skills 

<h3> Work Activities & Tasks: </h3>

- Share findings and collaborate with prosecutors 
- Create visual representations of data 
- Collaboration with other analysts to determine global violations of IUU fishing

<h3> Task Characteristics: </h3>

- There is high risk related to the tasks, as data cannot be deleted or misrepresented
- Tasks are performed frequently as many reports need to be generated simultaneously
- Tasks are often performed in collaboration with other data engineers/architects
- Tasks focus on streamlining data 
Information used: records of ship’s data, previous historical data, regulations from governmental agencies
- The time to complete task is critical, as it requires quick development of reports to aid in prosecution
- User is dedicated to the task at hand (limited multitasking)

<h3> Needs Related to the New System: </h3>

- Ability to synthesize reports and wrangle data using 2 platforms at maximun.
- Ability to communicate effectively with prosecution
- Automation with generation of reports

<h1> Role 3: CSO of Commercial Fishing Companies </h1>

<h3> Purpose and Goals: </h3>

- Ensure sustainable fishing
- Comply with regulations

<h3> Skills & Skill Requirements: </h3>

- Monitoring internal fishing activities
- Develop sustainable fishing practices within their company 

<h3> Work Activities & Tasks: </h3>

- Fishery assessment and certification
- Promotion/education of sustainable fishing practice
- Monitoring and reporting

<h3> Task Characteristics: </h3>

- Task is a relatively high risk, as the company is responsible for ensuring their commercial fishing practices are legal
- Tasks are performed frequently to ensure compliance and education
- Tasks are often performed in collaboration with others in commercial fishing to educate. 
- Tasks focus on using data and information to ensure sustainable fishing practices within their company. 
- Information used: records of ship’s data, previous historical data, regulations from governmental agencies
- The time to complete the task is critical, as commercial fishing vessels need to be educated on ever-changing fishing regulations. 
- User is multitasking and has to coordinate with multiple fishing and supply chain partners to ensure compliance. 

<h3> Needs Related to the New System: </h3>

- Ability to view where fishing is illegal
- Knowledge of specific fishing vessels violating the system
