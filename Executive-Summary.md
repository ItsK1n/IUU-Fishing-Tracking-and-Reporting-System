Illegal, unreported, and unregulated (IUU) fishing threatens marine sustainability, causing overfishing and threatening 3 billion people that depend on fish as their main source of protein. Additionally, the global economy faces economic losses estimated up to 36 billion dollars. 

To address this, we propose leveraging existing technologies such as AIS (Automatic Identification System), VMS (Vessel Monitoring System) tracking, and satellite imagery to monitor illegal fishing in real-time. In addition, we propose a unified communication platform allowing stakeholders to receive real-time updates on various fishing vessels, along with sharing data with other stakeholders. This is backed by a secure privacy authentication, ensuring only relevant stakeholders can access specific data. 

**Our solution prioritizes:**

- Tracking System: AIS and VMS provide a foundation for real-time location data monitoring vessel movements and detecting unauthorized activities. 
- Imagery Tracking: Satellite Imagery enhances coverage, allowing detection of vessels not using AIS or VMS.
- Automated Alerts: Real-time alerts will be sent out to local guards/authorities when suspicious activities are detected. 
- Centralized Database and Communication Platform: Allows stakeholders to communicate with other teams and stay up to date on information.


We achieve our primary objective by meeting these core priorities: to track and monitor illegal fishing activities and promote responsible fishing practices. This approach ensures a reduction in IUU fishing activities as response time by authorities is reduced, protection of marine biodiversity, and the long-term viability of the fishing industry. Eventually, we aim to raise public awareness and provide education on marine conservation, safeguarding the future of our oceans.
