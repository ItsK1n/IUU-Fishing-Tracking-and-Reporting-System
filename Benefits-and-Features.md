Based on stakeholder interviews and market research, we have identified the following high-priority features for the Illegal Fishing Tracking Information System (IFTIS).

**1. Automated vessel tracking**
- Description: Automatically detects any fishing vessels that disable their tracking devices within one minute of the event.
- Benefit: Enables quicker response from enforcement agencies, significantly reducing illegal fishing operations. This efficiency enhances our company's reputation for proactive compliance and leadership in sustainable marine practices.

**2. Automated alert system**
- Description: Deliver alerts that notify relevant stakeholders such as maritime enforcement agencies and regulatory officials via SMS or email when suspicious activities are detected within one minute.
- Benefit: Enhance the effectiveness of law enforcement and responses, ensuring quick reaction to protect marine areas, thus upholding our commitment to environmental protection and regulatory compliance.

**3. Real-Time Data Synchronization**
- Description: Syncs data from AIS every 30 seconds to provide up-to-date information on vessel movements.
- Benefit: Provides the most current data for precise tracking and swift actions against IUU activities, bolstering our capability to protect marine resources which is crucial for sustainable operations.

**4. Compliance Automation**
- Description: Automatically generates compliance reports that meet international and regional standards, ready for view and submission within an hour after data collection
- Benefit: Maintains high compliance rates with less effort and ensures accuracy in reporting

**5. Role-Based Data Access Control**
- Description: Implement a role-based access control system that defines clear permissions based on user roles. Data engineers will have read/write access to data. Data analysts and prosecutors will have read-only access.
- Benefit: Strengthens data security and integrity, aligning with our corporate policies on data protection and privacy, thus enhancing trust among stakeholders.

**6. Dynamic Data Dashboard**
- Description: An interactive dashboard that aggregates data from multiple sources, presenting it in an easy-to-understand format through charts and updates within 3 minutes.
- Benefit: Facilitates informed decision-making and strategic management of fishing resources, aligning with our goals to optimize resource utilization and environmental impact.

**7. Mobile Application Access**
- Description: Includes a mobile application that provides access to the system’s features, allowing users to monitor, receive alerts, and generate reports directly from their mobile devices
- Benefit: Increase the system’s accessibility and convenience, enabling stakeholders to stay informed and responsive even when away from work space.

**8. Data Encryption**
- Description: Implements high-level encryption for all data stored within the system, ensuring that all sensitive information is encrypted with the latest security protocols.
- Benefit: Protects sensitive information against unauthorized access, supporting our stringent security measures and maintaining stakeholder trust in our data handling capabilities.

**9. Customizable Reporting Tools**
- Description: Provides customizable reporting tools that allow users to generate specific reports based on selected criteria such as geographic areas or data ranges, with the capacity to complete and export reports within 5 minutes.
- Benefit: Offers personalized data analysis options, enhancing our ability to monitor specific trends and adapt our strategies effectively, thus driving operational efficiency and targeted action.
