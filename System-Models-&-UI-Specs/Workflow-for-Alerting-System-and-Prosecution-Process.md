**Alerting System and Prosecution Process Workflow**

The workflow diagram shows the process of detecting and prosecuting illegal fishing activities. Data Analysts initiate by analyzing patterns and developing visualizations to identify potential violations. Data Engineers then use this analysis to create and refine alerting software that promptly notifies Prosecutors of suspicious activities. Prosecutors review these alerts to decide on the appropriate legal actions. The system is deigned to ensure rapid response and accuracy, enhancing the enforcement of maritime regulations.
