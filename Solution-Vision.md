Current illegal fishing tracking systems face security vulnerabilities and require multiple platforms, hindering prosecution efforts. Moreover, 6% of fishing vessels tamper with satellite tracking devices, enabling them to evade detection and continue their illegal operations unchecked some even hiding in plain sight (Oceana, 2021). Our objective is to develop a secure global data system for tracking and recognizing boat activity patterns, enhancing prosecution capabilities against those participating in IUU fishing. Utilizing existing AIS and VMS tracking to in order to address evading detection and illegal fishing. We envision a unified platform that enhances security, allows unified data synthesis and visualization, clearly demonstrates IUU  violations to prosecution, and effectively displays IUU boundaries to fishing companies. 

**Solution** <br/>
- Database Creation: Establishing a secure unified and universally accessible database for analysts and prosecutors

- AIS Satellite Tracking: Utilizing AIS satellite tracking to monitor global ship movements, especially in IUU fishing-prone areas

- Alert System: Implementing an alert system for tracking system tampering or entry/exit of IUU hotspots

- Satellite Imagery: Integrating satellite imagery to capture a picture when alerts are triggered

- Pattern Recognition: Using fishing vessels data to create advance analytics for trends and pattern recognition, creating data-driven seasonal maps for alerting zones

- Unified Platform for Data Management and Visualization: Allows stakeholders to use one platform to analyze and create visuals to combat against IUU fishing

- Education Platform/Program: To inform and teach about sustainable fishing and promoting awareness to direct and indirect stakeholders

**Target Audience/Users** <br/>
- Commercial Fishing Companies
- Marine Conservation Organizations
- Legal Enforcement
- Data Analysts
- Local Communities
- Fishermen and Fishing Industry Workers

**Benefits** <br/> The new implementation is expected to provide the following benefits:
- Improves marine life conservation by reporting those responsible for detrimental impacts of IUU fishing, thus mitigating IUU activities
- Improves economic factors which the World Wildlife Fund estimates could save $36.4 billion annually (World Wildlife Fund).
- Improving careers of the fishermen by reducing IUU practices of forced labor and human rights violations within fishing fleets
- Improve the food scarcity of those local communities that depends on fishing as their main source of food
- Allow for data protection and ease of sharing between data analysts and prosecutors. 

**Alignment** <br/>
- Supports ways to track and monitor unlawful fishing practices
- Encourages sustainable marine life fishing practices
- Promotes data-driven policy helping enhance conservation strategies
