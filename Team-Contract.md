## Peer Feedback:
- Team members should allocate 10 minutes to give peer feedback at the beginning of the meeting on Friday to allow for consistent feedback and prevent further issues. Constructive feedback should include the “sandwich method”, embedding constructive feedback within compliments.

## Conflict Resolution Process:
1. Determine the severity of the conflict and see if it can be resolved informally or if a formal resolution process is necessary.
1. Arrange a meeting with people involved in the conflict. Have a neutral third party in the meeting if possible.
1. Establish ground rules for the meeting, such as one person speaking at a time and a commitment to seeking a resolution.
1. Define the issues that need to be resolved during the meeting. Encourage both sides to suggest solutions.
1. If consensus is challenging, consider voting on solutions with the understanding that the majority’s decision will be respected.
1. If no agreement can be established between the parties professors will be involved as a final resolution
