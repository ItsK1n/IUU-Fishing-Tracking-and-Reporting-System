To ensure a focused development process, the following is what will not be done as part of the solution for the Illegal Tracking Information System:

1. **Physical Tracking Development**
The solution will not include the development of physical tracking devices or hardware. It will utilize existing vessel tracking technologies such as the AIS provided by third parties.
2. **Direct Enforcement Action**
The system is designed to alert and inform enforcement agencies but will not directly engage in legal actions. Enforcement responsibilities remain with the respective maritime authorities.
3. **Real-Time Global Coverage**
Achieving real-time monitoring for all global waters is technologically and financially unfeasible given current satellite and AIS coverage limitations. The system will prioritize areas with the highest impact and best available data. Also the system will not focus on monitoring smaller bodies of water like lakes, rivers, or small coastal areas where commercial fishing is minimal.
4. **Long-term Data Storage**
The system will store historical data for a limited time frame. Older data may be deleted from the system according to practical regulatory needs and storage capabilities.
5. **Non-Fishing Vessel Monitoring**
The system will not monitor vessels that are not classified as fishing vessels, such as passenger ships.
